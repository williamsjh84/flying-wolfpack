originEyebrow: How it started
originHeadline: We almost didn't go
originParagraph1: In 2022, we sat across from each other at the kitchen table with a Europe itinerary, two kids who were 7 and 5, and a spreadsheet that had become genuinely terrifying. Three months. Six countries. One carry-on each.
originParagraph2: The smart decision was to wait. Wait until they were older, easier, more appreciative. Wait until flights were cheaper. Wait until we had more money, more time, more certainty. We nearly listened to that voice.
originParagraph3: We didn't. And it was the best decision we've made as a family.
originParagraph4: Flying Wolfpack started as a way to document that trip — partly for ourselves, partly for the handful of families who emailed asking "how did you do that?" It grew into something we didn't expect — a real community of families who are done waiting.
